**Company:** ServiceNow

**Documentation:** Generative AI safety, guardrails, configuration, and administration

## Project Overview

AI Guardian helps organizations apply safety controls to generative AI interactions by evaluating prompts and responses against configured guardrails. Its capabilities include offensiveness detection, prompt injection detection, sensitive topic filtering, and support for additional guardrail capabilities through Guardrail Service Providers.

As a Senior Technical Writer at ServiceNow, I worked on the AI Guardian documentation as the product evolved. I authored new content, updated existing topics for product changes, addressed documentation feedback, corrected content gaps and inaccuracies, and improved the organization and consistency of related topics.

My work included both conceptual and task-based documentation for administrators configuring and managing generative AI guardrails.
